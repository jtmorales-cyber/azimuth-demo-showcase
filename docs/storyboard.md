## Journey Overview

The demo tells one story: **bureaucratic chaos → guided clarity → lifecycle empowerment**. The visitor experiences the problem viscerally (Gauntlet), meets the solution (NOVA Hub), then explores proof across four domains (MAPS, K.I.T., BASE, SCOUT). Each tool showcase follows the BAB (Before-After-Bridge) framework within a horizontal swipe carousel.

## S0: Boot Sequence

**Scroll Position:** 0% (auto-plays on page load)  
**Duration:** 2.5 seconds  
**Framework:** AIDA — Attention

### Visual

Dark background. The Azimuth "A" monogram resolves from darkness — translucent shell materializes with refractive highlights, amber core light ignites. Compass ring fades in around the logo.

### Audio

Silence → single low-frequency tone rising to ambient hum.

### Text

None. Pure visual.

### Transition

"A" dissolves into particles that scatter, revealing the Gauntlet corridor.

### Emotional State

Curiosity → anticipation. The visitor knows they're entering something intentional.

## S1: The Gauntlet

**Scroll Position:** 5–15%  
**Duration:** 8–12 seconds of idle drift, then CTA appears  
**Framework:** PAS — Problem + Agitate

### Visual

Camera drifts through a brutalist government corridor. Grayscale concrete. Drop-ceiling fluorescent grid. Numbered doors (1143, 207B, 4) that follow no logic. Corridors branch at wrong angles — Escher-like geometry that loops back on itself. Closed doors with wire-glass windows showing more corridors beyond.

The geometry is oppressive, claustrophobic. No curves, no warmth, no translucency. Everything is `MeshStandardMaterial` — opaque, heavy, institutional.

### Audio

Three layers: (1) bureaucratic murmur (indistinct counter voices, hold music), (2) mechanical drone (HVAC, fluorescent hum, 60Hz buzz), (3) paper shuffle (stamps, forms, arrhythmic).

All low-pass filtered. The system isn't attacking you — it's ignoring you.

### Text (Glass-morphic overlay, appears at scroll 12%)

> **1.9 million veterans** navigate this system every year.  
> Average wait: **125 days**. Denial rate: **67%**.  
> The system wasn't built for you to understand it.

### CTA (appears at scroll 15%)

**"Find Your Bearing."** — Single amber-glow button, center frame. The first warm color in the entire experience.

### Emotional State

Frustration → recognition ("I know this place") → motivation to escape.

### NOVA Voice Design Note

NOVA is absent here. The Gauntlet represents the world *before* NOVA. No AI, no guidance, no help. The visitor feels the gap that Mission Mentor fills.

## S2: The Transition

**Trigger:** Tap "Find Your Bearing" (or continue scrolling past 15%)  
**Duration:** 3–4 seconds  
**Framework:** BAB — Bridge (the transformation moment)

### Visual

The concrete doesn't fade — it **fails**. Hairline fractures race across walls in geometric patterns following the ceiling grid and door frames. For one second, the fracture pattern is the most beautiful thing in the Gauntlet.

Fragments separate and lift. Each shifts from concrete gray to luminous cyan. The particle conversion is the visual thesis: bureaucracy's raw material, liberated from rigid structure, becomes navigational light.

Camera accelerates forward through dissolving corridors. Cyan particle streams rush past. The space opens. At center, a warm amber point ignites — NOVA's core.

A subtle perspective grid materializes beneath — grounding the scene. Compass rings assemble concentrically from NOVA. Brand geometry nodes arrange at cardinal positions on the grid. The Hub assembles with purpose.

### Audio

Bass drop clears all Gauntlet audio. Half-second near-silence. Then warm ambient pad fades in — wide, expansive, liberating.

### Text

None during transition. Pure experiential.

### Emotional State

Liberation. The most powerful 4 seconds in the demo. Everything that follows is proof of this promise.

## S5: MAPS Showcase (Pre-Enlistment)

**Scroll Position:** 45–55%  
**Interaction:** Horizontal swipe carousel — 4 cards  
**Brand Geometry:** Dodecahedron  
**Color Temperature:** Warm gold dominant, cyan accents  
**Audio:** Ascending warm harmonics, golden-hour ambience  
**Framework:** BAB per card

### Card 1: The Problem (Before)

**Visual:** Gauntlet-gray tint, a confused figure at a branching corridor of career options  
**Headline:** "Choosing Your Path Shouldn't Feel Like Guessing"  
**Body:** 200+ military career fields. No clear way to know which fits your strengths, interests, or goals. Most recruits choose based on a conversation — not data.  
**Metric:** Only **38%** of service members say their MOS matched their expectations.

### Card 2: The Solution (Bridge)

**Visual:** MAPS interface mockup — career pathway visualization with ASVAB alignment  
**Headline:** "MAPS Matches You to Careers That Actually Fit"  
**Body:** AI-powered aptitude alignment, branch/MOS recommendation, and career trajectory modeling — before you sign anything.  
**Feature Highlights:** ASVAB predictor, branch comparison, career path simulator

### Card 3: The Outcome (After)

**Visual:** Warm golden glow, dodecahedron fully illuminated  
**Headline:** "Enter Service With Eyes Open"  
**Metric Grid:**  
- **Aptitude match score** provided before enlistment  
- **Career path visibility** across 5, 10, 20 year horizons  
- **Branch comparison** data for informed decisions  

### Card 4: NOVA's Take

**Visual:** NOVA avatar (small particle sphere) with typewriter text  
**NOVA Voice:**  
> "Most people pick their MOS based on a recruiter's pitch and a 30-minute conversation. MAPS gives you the data to make that decision like it matters — because it does. This is the first 20 years of your career we're talking about."

### Transition Out

Scroll past → dodecahedron recedes, carousel fades, cube (K.I.T.) approaches.

## S7: BASE Showcase (Wellness)

**Scroll Position:** 65–75%  
**Interaction:** Horizontal swipe carousel — 4 cards  
**Brand Geometry:** Sphere  
**Color Temperature:** Warm gradient blend (soft purple, sage green) with warm amber safe-space glow  
**Audio:** Delta wave (2Hz binaural), warm low-frequency, breathing-synchronized  
**Framework:** BAB per card — with trauma-informed care principles (per nova-safeguard)

### Card 1: The Problem (Before)

**Headline:** "The People Who Need Help Most Are the Least Likely to Ask for It"  
**Body:** Stigma. Distrust. The fear that seeking help means weakness. 60% of veterans with mental health conditions don't seek treatment. The system's design doesn't help — clinical intake forms, impersonal interfaces, zero cultural competence.  
**Metric:** **60%** of veterans with mental health needs don't access care.

### Card 2: The Solution (Bridge)

**Visual:** BASE interface mockup — whole health check-in, warm and non-clinical  
**Headline:** "BASE Meets You Where You Are — Not Where the System Wants You"  
**Body:** Whole-health check-ins across mental, physical, and spiritual domains. Culturally competent. No clinical jargon. Confidential. Connects to human support when it matters.  
**Feature Highlights:** Whole health domains, crisis pathway detection, peer support connection, wellness tracking

### Card 3: The Outcome (After)

**Visual:** Warm gradient glow, sphere radiating protective warmth  
**Headline:** "A Safe Space That's Always Open"  
**Metric Grid:**  
- **Confidential** check-ins on your schedule  
- **Crisis detection** that connects, doesn't diagnose  
- **Whole health** across all domains — not just mental  

### Card 4: NOVA's Take

**NOVA Voice:**  
> "BASE doesn't pretend to be a therapist. It's the check-in you actually do — because it respects your time, your privacy, and your agency. When something needs human attention, BASE connects you. That's it. No lectures."

### Design Note: Trauma-Informed Principles Applied

- No clinical language or medical imagery
- Warm color temperature communicates safety, not sterility
- Audio uses calming delta waves — longest crossfade of any showcase (3s)
- Content validates without pitying (empathy, not sympathy — per NOVA persona)
- Crisis detection mentioned as capability, not demonstrated

## S8: SCOUT Showcase (VA Claims)

**Scroll Position:** 75–85%  
**Interaction:** Horizontal swipe carousel — 4 cards  
**Brand Geometry:** Octahedron  
**Color Temperature:** Deep blue dominant, silver analytical accents  
**Audio:** Precision hum, systematic processing tones, confirmation chimes  
**Framework:** BAB per card (strongest PAS application — highest-pain use case)

### Card 1: The Problem (Before)

**Headline:** "You Served Your Country. Then You Waited 125 Days for an Answer."  
**Body:** 1.9M disability claimants. 67% initially denied. Not because they don't qualify — because the paperwork isn't complete. Missing one document triggers a 6-month appeal. While you wait, you can't plan financially. The stress compounds.  
**Metric:** **67%** initial denial rate. **125-day** average wait.

### Card 2: The Solution (Bridge)

**Visual:** SCOUT interface mockup — claim readiness dashboard with green/yellow/red indicators  
**Headline:** "SCOUT Optimizes Your Claim Before You Submit"  
**Body:** Upload documents once. AI analyzes against VA rules and 500K+ successful claims. See exactly what's missing. Get specific optimization recommendations. Generate a VA-compliant claim in 20 minutes.  
**Feature Highlights:** AI claims parser, evidence strength meter, optimization engine, compliant claim generator

### Card 3: The Outcome (After)

**Visual:** Octahedron fully illuminated face by face, precision data lattice  
**Headline:** "From 33% Odds to 90%. In 20 Minutes."  
**Metric Grid:**  
- **90%** approval rate (vs. 33% average)  
- **20 minutes** to optimized claim (vs. 125-day wait)  
- **$4.99** — less than a cup of coffee to claim what you earned  

### Card 4: NOVA's Take

**NOVA Voice:**  
> "SCOUT doesn't file your claim for you. It makes sure your claim is ready before you file it. Every document checked. Every gap identified. Every recommendation based on what actually gets approved. The VA doesn't deny qualified veterans — it denies incomplete paperwork. SCOUT fixes that."
